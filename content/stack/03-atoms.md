Atoms are the physical substrate: the compute, the GPUs, the racks, the machines the intelligence actually runs on. This is the layer that decides whether your AI is sovereign or rented.

When your firm's operations run on intelligence you rent, you carry a silent counterparty risk: the spigot can be repriced, rate-limited, or turned off, and none of it is your decision. Dedicated compute you control, running open models you can audit, removes that risk at the root.

Railways were the infrastructure layer of the last industrial transition. Whoever laid the rails set the terms for everyone who shipped on them. I grew up in a railroad town. I am back to laying rails.
