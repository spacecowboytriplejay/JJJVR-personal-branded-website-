Intelligence is the top of the stack: the models themselves. Frontier models, open models, the reasoning engines that everything else exists to serve.

Most firms rent this layer by the token and stop there. That is like renting an engine and calling yourself a railroad. A model alone is not a system. It becomes one when it is wired into harnesses, given tools, put inside loops, and pointed at a business constraint with judgment wired in at the top.

The gap between what frontier models can do and what most firms actually deploy is the largest arbitrage in business today. Closing that gap is the work.
