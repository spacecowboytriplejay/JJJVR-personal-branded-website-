## Work with me

There are four interfaces.

**Read.** The essays are free, forever. [Subscribe](/#essays) and you have the highest-bandwidth channel to how I think.

**Build.** If you are a founder or firm that wants frontier AI systems or private AI infrastructure, apply through [SelfBuiltSystems](https://www.selfbuiltsystems.com). Do not pitch me tooling demos. Bring a business constraint.

**Invest.** If you are raising or co-investing, read the essays first. A pitch that references the thesis it is answering gets read. A cold deck does not.

**Advise.** An advisory call is $1,000 per hour. The price is a filter, not a revenue line: it guarantees that both of us show up with a problem worth solving. Come with one specific decision, constraint, or system.
